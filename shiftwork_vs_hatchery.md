## Decisions

- **Provisional recommendation: Shiftwork should replace Hatchery as the main project** — its learner action, sandbox execution, deterministic evaluation, persistent world state, and visible outcome can form one coherent mechanic. Hatchery has potentially broader consumer impact, but its practice layer may be unnecessary beside strong fixed assistants and its real-world integrations create greater proof and safety burdens.
- **Shiftwork's proposed definition:** a persistent applied-AI-engineering town whose businesses run on the learner's actual deployed systems. Later chapters inherit the code, data, incidents, business state, and relationships produced by earlier work.
- **Central mechanic:** learner decision -> sandbox build -> scripted customers plus independent tests -> deterministic business ledger -> inherited next chapter. The town is an integration test that remembers, not a map of lessons.

- Hatchery's outcome overlaps [Ohai](https://www.ohai.ai/) and [Kynlo](https://kynlo.life/). Its teach/test/improve mechanism overlaps Claude skill-creator, Hermes self-evolution, Understudy, and Skills Dojo. A strong fixed household procedure remains a required baseline.

## Traps

- **Temptation: call the category novel.** Career simulation, gamified AI learning, executable labs, AI feedback, and portfolio proof are already served. Only the combined causal persistence mechanism remains a plausible gap.
- **Temptation: let an LLM narrate consequences.** Customer incidents, correctness, cost, latency, refunds, and business state must follow reproducibly from executions and fixed rules or the central claim collapses.
- **Temptation: let Nemotron complete its own curriculum.** Nemotron may construct scaffolding, characters, environments, and bounded hints; the learner must retain meaningful problem-definition, tradeoff, evaluation, and deployment decisions.
- **Temptation: count generated tests as independent.** Nemotron must not control hidden cases, the evaluator, scoring, the world ledger, or promotion rules.
- **Temptation: make Tavily a lesson-text refresher.** Its source discovery must change an executable constraint, test, or world behaviour and beat a strong frozen-curriculum or known-page-polling baseline. Published missions require cited, reviewed, frozen versions.
- **Temptation: treat pixel art, XP, buildings, or an avatar as differentiation.** Visible progression must be caused by working software and inherited consequences.
- **Temptation: claim 35 from the concept.** No application, user test, transfer result, account entitlement, or learning benefit has been demonstrated.
- **Temptation: hide the sponsor-dependence conflict.** Shiftwork appears more architecturally dependent on inference plus sandbox execution than Hatchery, but another model and sandbox provider could still substitute for Nebius.
