Shift brief

“Help customers find books and understand our policies without inventing certainty.”

  • First: decide what a safe, useful answer means.
  • Then: write one check a customer answer must pass.
  • During the shift: run your service against the customer queue.
  • After an incident: change the artifact and keep the decision on record.

What makes a later shift different

The next shift inherits this exact artifact, your decisions, the incidents it caused, and its unresolved limits. Progress comes from changing the working service—not from points or badges.
