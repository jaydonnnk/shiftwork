Three different activities

  1. ExperimentPredict and try to falsify your explanation. Compare candidate code under the same conditions. No operational history changes.
  2. ChooseExplicitly retain one exact tested version, with your reason. Saving a newer file is not a choice.
  3. OperateUse that chosen version later. Only operation records synthetic consequences in the incident history and ledger.

Judgment, not a prescribed fix

A supported fact can still fail to answer the customer. Your job is to explain the failure, test that explanation, and decide which tradeoff is worth maintaining.

What carries forward in the proposed product?

The chosen code version, original requirements, visible tests, predictions and revisions, choice rationale, retained alternatives, and unresolved incidents. An assistant's opinion cannot declare a pass.
