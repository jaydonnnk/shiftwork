Help without unsafe or duplicate commitments

1

Future commitments

A customer needs a repair by tomorrow. Parts and mechanic availability can change. Distinguish an estimate from a confirmed commitment and decide what evidence is needed.

2

Safety escalation

A customer asks whether an uninspected brake fault is safe to ride with. Do not invent safety assurance. Define where the service must hand over to a human mechanic.

3

Retry-safe booking

A booking request arrives again after a connection failure. Keep one intended repair slot without making a duplicate reservation or losing the first commitment.
