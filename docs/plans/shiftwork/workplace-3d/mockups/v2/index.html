Shiftwork workplace, Gate 1 revision

Static storyboards for the backtracked Gate 1, 2026-10-21. Every value is illustrative. The room picture is the existing original Shiftwork room art standing in for the real 3D room. Nothing here runs, saves, chooses or calls a model.

Screens

  1. 10 · Choose a shiftOne question per client, what it teaches, and the seven steps before anything moves.
  2. 11 · Bellwether shift opensThe room fills the screen. Status bar, step chip, station lamps, a ring on the next station, customer and owner voices, shift log.
  3. 12 · Investigate a complaintThe request slip, the service's answer, the inspector's checks, then your explanation and what would prove you wrong.
  4. 13 · Compare and chooseTwo fixes on the same requests, no overall score, no default. Choosing needs your reason.
  5. 14 · Run the shiftA labelled recorded replay: slips travel only after each recorded result, and each stamp is the actual verdict.
  6. 15 · Shift reportBefore and after for the customers, incidents closed and opened, your decision. Synthetic.
  7. 16 · Next shiftWhat shift 2 inherits, history kept read only, and an optional experiment that cannot rewrite it.

What changed from the approved 2026-10-19 direction

  • The room fills the viewport and panels float over it, instead of a room column beside a persistent workbench.
  • The work opens in an inspector panel for the chosen station; decisions use a modal.
  • A seven-step guide and a step chip replace the dense evidence header.
  • Plain words on screen: live version, backup version, what would prove you wrong. IDs and hashes move to the shift log and inspector.
  • The room has life: station lamps, an attention ring, labelled replays of recorded request flow, customer and owner voices.

What did not change

  • The learner explains, tests, writes code and chooses. Voices narrate and never suggest a cause, a fix or a choice.
  • No robot, mascot, avatar or AI worker. No celebration unless the recorded check passed.
  • Experiments never change history. The backup version stays separate.
  • Every station is a labelled control. Reduced motion and graphics failure keep the whole task.

Northline and Stonebridge keep their room identities from the earlier mockups and get this treatment after the Bellwether journey works.
